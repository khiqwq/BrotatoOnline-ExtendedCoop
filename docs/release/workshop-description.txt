标题：
超四人联机合作扩展 | 5-16 Player Co-op for Brotato Online

说明（粘贴到创意工坊页面“编辑标题和描述”，支持 BBCode）：

[h1]超四人联机合作扩展[/h1]
让 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3741034628]Brotato Online[/url] 支持 5～16 人联机合作，每台电脑一人。

[b]测试版 0.1.0：[/b]已在单机模拟中走通 5～16 人的完整流程（选人、战斗、升级、商店、结算、退出后继续），尚未在多台电脑真实联机中验证。遇到问题请到下方源代码页面反馈。

[h2]需要[/h2]
[list]
[*]Brotato 1.2.0.1（内置 Mod Loader 6.3.0）
[*]订阅并启用 Brotato Online 2026-11-04 更新版（网络协议 6.3.0）
[*]扩展房间里的每个人都要订阅本扩展
[/list]

[h2]用法[/h2]
[list]
[*]房主在角色选择界面的运行选项里选房间类型：
[list]
[*][b]标准房间（4 人，原版兼容）[/b]：没装本扩展的玩家也能加入，一切与原版相同。
[*][b]扩展房间（最多 16 人）[/b]：5～16 人都能开；所有成员都要装本扩展；没装的玩家会看到“版本不兼容”而无法加入。
[/list]
[*]商店、升级、结算界面：本机面板放大，旁边是其他玩家的状态条（武器、选购状态）；用手柄选中某个玩家可以展开他的商店、武器和道具。鼠标点击需要同时启用 CoopFix-OnlineMouseUI。
[*]按 V 或手柄 Back 键切换到原版式的四人视图，用左右方向键、右摇杆或十字键左右滑动查看其他玩家；再按一次切回。
[*]9 人以上：战斗 HUD 变成细血条、细经验条和小号材料数字；商店、升级、结算和选人界面的玩家列表一次显示 7 人（结算界面 6 人），鼠标指针放在列表上滚动滚轮、推右摇杆上下，或把光标移过首行/末行即可翻看。在“设置 → 游戏操作”里选择整页翻动（默认）或逐个滚动。
[*]5 人以上的局退出游戏后，可以从标题界面“继续”。
[*]5 人以上时场地按人数放大（保持 4 人时的人均面积，8 人 45×34 格，16 人 64×48 格）；敌人数量、生命、伤害与材料沿用原版的人数缩放。
[*]DLC：扩展房间里，每位玩家的面板上显示他安装的 DLC 图标，灰色表示没装。没装 DLC 的玩家也能玩开了 DLC 的局（由 Brotato Online 支持）。
[/list]

[h2]兼容性[/h2]
[list]
[*]不修改 Brotato Online 的任何文件。
[*]只认 Brotato Online 2026-11-04 更新版（网络协议 6.3.0）（锁定文件指纹）。Brotato Online 或游戏更新后，本扩展会自动关闭超四人功能，Brotato Online 照常以 4 人运行，等本扩展更新后再恢复。
[*]已验证组合：Brotato 1.2.0.1（DLC 已并入游戏本体），Mod Loader 6.3.0，Brotato Online 2026-11-04 更新版（网络协议 6.3.0），可选 CoopFix-OnlineMouseUI 1.0.1。
[*]性能：房主的电脑负担最重。在一台笔记本 RTX 5070 上单机模拟、每人带满后期炮台套装时，12 人后期约 94～163 FPS，14 人最低约 40 FPS，16 人最后几波在约 6～50 FPS 之间（随场上敌人数量波动）。
[*]每台电脑一名玩家；本地（同屏）合作仍最多 4 人。
[/list]

[h2]已知限制[/h2]
[list]
[*]尚未在多台电脑联机中测试。
[*]房主移除玩家后，其余玩家按原顺序重新分配（单机模拟验证）；断线后可回到原来的位置（沿用 Brotato Online 的流程）。两者都还没有在多台电脑上实测。
[*]5 人以上的局不能用“离线继续”。
[/list]

[h1]5-16 Player Co-op for Brotato Online[/h1]
Extends [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3741034628]Brotato Online[/url] co-op to 5-16 players, one player per PC.

[b]Test release 0.1.0:[/b] the full 5-16 player flow (character selection, waves, upgrades, shop, end of run, continue after quitting) works in local simulation; it has not yet been tested online across several PCs.

[list]
[*]Requires Brotato 1.2.0.1 (Mod Loader 6.3.0) and the 4 Nov 2026 update of Brotato Online (network protocol 6.3.0). Everyone in an extended room needs this mod.
[*]Pick the room type in the run options on character selection: Standard (4, vanilla-compatible) or Extended (up to 16; it runs with 5 players too).
[*]Shop / upgrades / end of run: your panel is large, the others are shown as status rows; select a player to open their panel. Press V or gamepad Back for the vanilla-style four-panel view and slide with the arrow keys, right stick or D-pad.
[*]With 9+ players the battle HUD uses thin bars and small material counts, and the player list shows 7 players at a time (6 on the end-of-run screen): scroll it with the mouse wheel over it, the right stick, or by moving the cursor past its first/last row. Settings > Gameplay chooses page by page (default) or one row at a time.
[*]Performance depends on the host: in local simulation on a laptop RTX 5070 with a full late-game turret kit per player, 12 players stay around 94-163 FPS in late waves, 14 dip to about 40, and 16 swing between about 6 and 50 FPS in the last waves depending on how many enemies are alive.
[*]With 5+ players the arena grows with the player count (same area per player as with 4; 45x34 tiles for 8). Enemy count, HP, damage and materials follow the base game's player scaling.
[*]DLC: in an extended room each player's panel shows the icons of their installed DLCs, grey when missing. Players without a DLC can still play a run with it enabled (Brotato Online handles this).
[*]Brotato Online files are not modified. If Brotato Online or the game updates, the mod switches its 5+ player features off and Brotato Online runs unchanged until this mod is updated.
[*]Removing a player re-seats the others in order (verified in local simulation); a disconnected player can return to their seat (Brotato Online's own flow). Neither has been tested across several PCs yet.
[/list]
